Downgrading or cancelling your account

If you cancel your subscription or remove your phone verification, you will be notified how many saved recipes you will lose access to before any changes are made. You will always keep your most recently saved recipes up to your new tier limit. Ratings and comments you have left on recipes always remain visible publicly. Phone numbers can only be changed once every 30 days.

All recipes ever uploaded to Curated Kitchen remain on the site regardless of account status. Deleting your account does not remove your uploaded recipes, comments, reviews, or recipe photos from the platform.
